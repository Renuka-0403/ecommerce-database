# Helios Watch Store E-Commerce Database

## Overview
This repository contains the Business Requirements Analysis (BRA) and Requirement Analysis documents for the **Helios Watch Store E-Commerce Database** project. The project focuses on designing a relational database for a multi-brand online watch store to efficiently manage business operations and business data.

## Problem Statement
The rapid growth of e-commerce has increased the need for efficient management of customer information, watch products, inventory, orders, payments, and other business data. As a multi-brand watch retailer, Helios Watch Store manages a large volume of information related to multiple watch brands, product categories, customers, transactions, and inventory. Managing this data manually or through unstructured methods can result in data redundancy, inconsistencies, delayed order processing, inventory inaccuracies, and difficulties in retrieving and maintaining information.

To overcome these challenges, this project aims to design a structured relational database that efficiently organizes and manages business data while ensuring data integrity, reducing redundancy, and supporting the core operations of the online watch store.

## Project Description
The proposed database is designed to manage the following information:
- Customer Management
- Brand Management
- Product Management
- Category Management
- Inventory Management
- Order Management
- Payment Management
- Shipment Management
- Review Management
- Warranty Management

The project applies relational database concepts, normalization techniques, and entity relationships to design a structured and efficient database for an e-commerce watch store.
